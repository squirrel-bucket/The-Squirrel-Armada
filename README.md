**The Squirrel Armada**

_A 2D platformer featuring bee-knights, drones, waffles, and a rather talkative armchair._

**About the Project**

The Squirrel Armada is a 2D platformer developed as a personal project to explore Unity and game development. Players fight their way through themed levels, facing off against bee-knights and drones while collecting food to regenerate health and progressing through an increasingly strange world.

Originally started in late 2022 or early 2023, the project has grown into an ongoing exploration of game mechanics, animation, enemy behavior, level design, and interactive dialogue. Development continues as new ideas and systems are introduced.

**Features**

- **Combat and Enemy Encounters** — Battle bee-knights and drones, with additional enemy types planned for future updates.
- **Health Regeneration Collectibles** — Collect food items, including waffles and pumpkin pie, to restore health.
- **Custom Animations** — Handcrafted animations for the player, enemies, checkpoints, and level transitions.
- **Interactive Dialogue** — Meet a sentient armchair whose dialogue changes between levels.
- **Themed Levels** — Explore distinct environments, with additional settings planned for future updates.
- **Ongoing Development** — New mechanics, story elements, enemies, and environments are planned as the project evolves.

**Controls**

WASD to move, SPACE to jump, SHIFT to sprint, and Left Mouse Button to attack

**Technology**

- **Game Engine**: Unity
- **Programming Approach**: Unity Visual Scripting
- **Genre**: 2D Platformer

_The project was originally developed around 2022–2023. It has since been shifted to be compatible with the 6000.6.5f1 editor version._

**Development Roadmap**

The project is actively being developed. Planned improvements include:

- Expand the enemy roster beyond bee-knights and drones.
- Develop a more defined overarching storyline.
- Introduce two additional major settings beyond the current one.
- Continue expanding the game's mechanics, animations, and level content.
- These goals may evolve as development progresses.

**Project Goals**

The Squirrel Armada began as an opportunity to learn Unity through hands-on experimentation. Rather than limiting the project to isolated tutorials, development has involved combining gameplay systems, animation, visual scripting, collectibles, checkpoints, level transitions, and dialogue into a single evolving game.

The project serves as an ongoing exercise in game design, technical problem-solving, and iterative development.
